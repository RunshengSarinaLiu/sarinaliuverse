Stories

Sarina Liu studied Computer Science and Political Science at Vanderbilt University, with a deep curiosity about finance, art, and cultural markets.

One of her favorite thinkers is the architect Louis Kahn, whose words, “Your intuition is your greatest gift,” resonate deeply with her. Drawing on her interdisciplinary background, Sarina explores different ways we perceive and understand the world.

She believes in the power of inner expression. Through writing, photography, film, and multimedia storytelling, she seeks to capture and share a life that is vivid, poetic, and deeply spiritual.
